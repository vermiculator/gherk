whilst [[atomic content|atomic fragments]] should have the **capacity** to act equivalently / be treated as first order / hold any position in any hierarchy, they shouldn't be assumed to **be equivalent**. 

any fragment can have varying significance / importance / weight to its author / others who interact with it, they shouldn't be limited to balanced / side-by-side presentation
