a lot of research is finding the right person (people) to ask, routing through knowledge lineage to find people more representative, people more knowledgeable, experts in the particular facet of something you're interested in/from the angle you're interested in

i see this a lot in everyday information usage via eg search engines and now increasingly [[AI|chatbots (derogatory)]]

a lot of usage is 'i have a question on x, but don't know any x experts' and triaging useful study paths is a lot of the heavy lifting here
