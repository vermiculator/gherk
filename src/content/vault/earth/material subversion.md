always fascinated by something made out of the 'wrong' material or one material made to look/feel like another. it is part illusion, part performance, sometimes nostalgia, sometimes resistance, plays on ideas of ephemerality and robustness and expectations. can form new, more interesting mediums from the combination of the affordances of both.
