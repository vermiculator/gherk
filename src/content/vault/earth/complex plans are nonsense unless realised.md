letting people complete complex/dramatic/risky projects is mostly based on past experience and/or proven skill, so essentially on trust and charisma (and maybe charismatic friends)

the biggest and best plans are functionally impossible and essentially ridiculous - the more steps completed the more assured the finale is

this sounds straightforwardly obvious maybe but it is interesting to me. for everyone in any situation, literally nothing exists unless it does. small steps, capacity-building, etc, often determinant of plans then working out well enough
