usually I'm not a fan of linguistic drift towards less distinction between similar terms (feels more like anti-intellectualism than allowing multiple forms of meaning if it's to LESS distinction) but this one I don't mind, which interests me as to why -- the distinction is technologically defined but the merging is too - are the two terms still useful?? 

typeface is technically the set of fonts - a typeset

but now the variations of fonts as would make up a typeface can be expressed as one variable 'font'
