combining / grouping together / claiming equivalence over several independent elements declares their relation to each other - if this is over elements authored by / uploaded by / otherwise of interest to particular people / groups, it can only be just if it is included with respect to / with consent of / with agreement of / maybe even at the explicit request of the individuals / groups concerned

TLDR; your Yellow isn't the same as my Yellow unless we both agree to it

---

- the issue is exemplified in tagging where various communities use the same acronym or a single term is used very differently by different people
- not asking for consent often has consequence in generative AI - immoral work
- more widely, consuming an individual into a whole isn't a neutral act
- perhaps the only equivalence we should assume is that of one human and another with regards to rights and ethics
	- even this is contested ([[anti~fascism|fascism]]) or enacted in ways that are [[contextual, relational and situational]]
	- but it is more evenly debated as to how to equate other life forms ([[human-species supremacy]])
