term used in philosophy to refer to the 'language of thought' hypothesis - that thoughts aren't inherently a spoken/written/natural language as in those we use to communicate but a base form with its own structure. 

can it be considered a language, a grammar?
is it directly reflective of brain patterns or something more?
is it individual to each of us or shared?
is it shared across species, across time?
are there dialects, different modes, etc?
why do some people 'think in' words/images/feelings/whatever more than others?
