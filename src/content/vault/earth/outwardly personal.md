positioning the 'personal' as inherently relational, a deliberate exteriorisation of subjecthood

- alternative to personal as in private, or personal as in alone
- i view this as a more reflective and honest version of the 'personal branding' discussed in regards to online identity that pushes for marketable personas
- this view differs from the false authenticity of internet personas in that it necessitates self-truth to project out as deliberate, attentive subjecthood

- "a broader sense and meaning of the term “personal,”" [[Para-Archives - Rethinking Personal Archiving Practices in the Times of Capture Culture|(Smolicki, 2017)]]
- "invitation to apprehend the notion of the personal differently, not as implying a move inward (protection from the outside and the public), but also (and in today’s media technological landscape, perhaps inevitably) an attentive, reflective, and critical exteriorization of one’s subjecthood into para-archival traces."
