- what about those you leave behind? what about their next generation?
- the 'run away to a commune' response to existing under capitalism - short-sighted, works only for the already-privileged, creates silos of alternative reality disconnected from wider society
- any proto-society or alternative way of living must interface with the wider world 
- eg disabled people reliant on particular medications and devices can't opt out of global systems that produce, distribute, maintain and control these medications and devices. 
- any alternative systems exist in relation to and within global capitalism as default
