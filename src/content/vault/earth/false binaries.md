## instances
- sex, gender, or traits associated with them arbitrarily
    - male or female - social/political/biological/ontological realities are far more complex
    - XX or XY chromosomes - gene biology is more complex than this, other combinations exist or correspond otherwise 
    - penis or vagina - biology is more complex than this, clitoris == penis, intersex people exist
    - oestrogen or testosterone - we all have a mixture, dominance and levels vary 
    - hairy or smooth - we are all mammals, it varies by ethnicity and grooming habits
- morality
    - good vs evil - everyone wrestles with this constantly and a million people before and after me have discussed the grey areas
    - us vs them - same as above, but also with strong emphasis on [[oneness]]
- race and ethnicity
    - black vs white
        - not necessarily false, just used badly
        - most cases would do better with the phrasing 'racialised people' - [[Whiteness|construction of Whiteness]]
        - mixed people and light-skinned black people have varying experience 
        - European, Asian and Latinx people with darker skin have varying experience 
        - more useful concepts in many cases are ethnic minority, people of colour, people of the Global South
        - say Black people and White people if that's what you actually mean, obviously
- species
	- human/animal - [[human as animal]], [[deep ecology|anti human-species supremacy]]
	- predator/prey - [[interspecies relations]]
