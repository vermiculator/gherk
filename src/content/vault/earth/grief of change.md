* everything changes and everything ends
* constant little griefs, reflavouring anxieties
* covers all the same emotions as big grieving, just less of them
* times and places and people lost just through time moving on
* exacerbated by depression, anxiety, being autistic 
* exacerbated by fast-changing times
* exacerbated by lost time, [[queer time]], disabled time
