i don't believe a lot of the projects intending to hear plant music, see animal art etc are actually engaging with expression of other life as the creatures don't retain agency and can't confirm correct translation. non-consensual introduction of tech to animals often bothers me in ways i can't quite articulate

i love the video of the guy teaching an octopus to play piano and think there's a lot of insight in it but this octopus is still held in captivity and has little autonomy over the situation, it is difficult to say whether or not this is 'true' creative expression or mimicking or whatever

a relevant convo currently in more-than-human comms because of surge in LLM use, similar convos being had widely on 'intelligence' and creativity and consciousness and soul
