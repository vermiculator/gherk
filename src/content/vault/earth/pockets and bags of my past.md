title: pockets and bags of my past
---

* misogynistic context to the handbag
* resented the insinuation that women should carry not wallet-and-keys necessities in pockets but an arsenal of perfect preparedness for baby sick and smudged makeup.
* dad's 'man-bag'; more reminiscent of a cross-body tool pouch, this would signify that he was additionally invested in 'preparedness'
* in school, large haulage demands, fitting books most important but also should distribute weight
* naturally inclined to over-think and over-solve, saw an opportunity for better design
* saw sisters digging through handbag junk, myself ruined backpacks with exploded biros, squashed food remnants and leaky bottles
* in protest of both gendered expectations and the abysmal quality of bags available to me, I stuffed my tiny jeans pockets to bursting point until i made the switch to trousers designed for men
* feeling tethered and anchored by the valuables in my pockets
* still in need of accessibility, durability and comfort as well as space for pens and lunch
* some requirements at one time
	* Separate compartment for water bottle, easy access and no leakage
	* Accessible items- preferably zip opening from above, or easy clasps
	* Enough structure to hold shape, but soft enough to be comfortable to wear
	* Evenly weighted, storable and simple
* Valuables and Replaceables
  * hold-all type bag was really only useful for a bulk of items that all fit one category.
    * Extra clothes, toiletries, or groceries,
    * Common and functional
    * matters less to lose
    * Like goes with like so that things are easy to find and remain undamaged.
    * public transport and unwieldy luggage
* breast pocket valuables
* sacrificial items
* hence airplane hold luggage for example
* always-luggage, backpackers, split between houses/similar, homelessness, possessions constantly in tow, no multiples
* some only just too small for a water bottle
* the perceived flexibility of a bag this size is often inadequate in practice for any one purpose
* maybe perfect for slightly larger everyday items, eg full-sized deodorant or fold-up umbrella
  * non-traditional wallets, small pouch-type bags, ​modular sets for military use.
  * regular thick belt method weighty and irritating- even more so than pockets
  * 'handbag on suitcase'
  * 'stuffed pockets, stuffed backpack'
* bus etc, irritating to take backpack off to sit down, and backpack feels safe
* decreasing need for lunch-bag style bags or those that accommodate lunch boxes inside
* open bag as toolbox, portable static storage
* padding/pillow integrated luggage
* pockets accessible whilst wearing bags, range of motion
* under-clothing storage
* wfh office design
* [robot delivery](https://www.starship.xyz/business/) may evolve into an automated porter service
* life changes and bag changes
