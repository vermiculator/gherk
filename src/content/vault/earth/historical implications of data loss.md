archival instability and immaturity for digitally native content or networked assemblages has dramatic impact on the nature of archival/information science, on the kinds/amounts of historical record available from certain times

early eras of tech that later becomes ubiquitous is often poorly archived as techniques haven't caught up or forms are more instable

entire periods of historical information are lost to this. how in the moment do we ever determine what is worth archiving? some things are archived by default eg if they are to do with nation governance but everyday information is ephemeral by default for better and worse

implications on legal proceedings when people involved use novel/unusual methods of communication

early internet is largely not archived.

business needs often prioritised over everything, including harm, care, historical value -- eg copyright law eg YouTube taking down the original 'rick-roll'
