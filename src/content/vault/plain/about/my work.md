- i do:
	- human-centred design for (minimal) tech
	- queer-anarchistic approach to tech development
- i study:
	- the tensions and blurred boundaries between private, public, personal and social information practises
	- the handling of uncertainties, irrationalities and unknowables as valuable data under flexible frameworks
	- how intersubjective counter-narratives form counter-publics
	- tactics of resistance within the everyday and amongst the noise
	- capture, curation and para-archival
	- second brains and tools for thought
	- the digital humanities, taxonomies and linked data
- i prioritise:
	- [[anti~capitalism|anti-capitalism]] and [[de~colonialism|decolonisation]]
	- [[ethics of care]] 
	- [[pluriversality]]
	- human agency against [[anti~auto-equivalence|auto-equivalence]] and [[nonconsensual combination]]
- towards:
	- robust tech policy
	- [[permacomputing]]
	- [[digital commons]]
	- tangible interfacing
	- collective liberation
