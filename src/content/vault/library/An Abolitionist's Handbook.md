## What Is a Handbook?

why this format
- "This book is not meant to prove to someone who you are - or who you aren't"
- actively encourages you to annotate and share
- (I did initially annotate directly in the book and type these up so they were linked with other notes but could still pass it on in the spirit of its instruction)
- "questions and reminders for yourself and others" - [[the pseudo-universal human]] or [[a universal universal]]
- "I don't need you to always agree with my thoughts here. If I've done my job, you *won't* agree with every line" - [[learn from disparate sources]]
- specific recommendations, themes along their wavelength
- not a memoir
- stressing fallibility and human learning, not claiming expertise
- 'I get up and try every day.'

guidelines

- Nothing Is Fixed
- Say Yes to Imagination

- Allow Yourself to Feel

## Courageous Conversations
- "I was learning from a very early age to not get emotional" - the little traumas you learn without intention, eg parent body language
- "Sometimes we have to go to a deep internal space for a conversation" - this is why i have to write rather than speak to get things out in an important conversation - my deep internal self doesn't really speak. 
- "Parents from my mum's generation weren't given the tools for courageous conversations"
- so we learn the tools, we hope they can learn them too, and we make sure to teach them to our children
- "Whether or not she was being pushed to tell me or she just wanted me to know, she broke through and spoke to me."
- "I needed more than just courage to have this conversation with her. I needed several therapy sessions and some pep talks from my friends" - and thats ok!!
- Anne Lamott - "You own everything that happened to you. Tell your stories. If people wanted you to write warmly about them, they should've behaved better." - this is tricky. "My mum did everything she could to be a good mum." - what do you really 'own'? - multiple realities to any one relationship or situation, there's no objective truth, 'defamation', warped memory, delusion...
